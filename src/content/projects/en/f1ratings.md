sub: "XRating: ratings for F1 drivers and teams"
---
My own rating system for Formula 1 drivers and teams. It tries to give somewhat representative ratings and to avoid the rating inflation common in other systems. Built on the Kaggle Formula 1 Race Data dataset.

## Features

- Driver and team ratings, current and all-time
- Tuned against rating inflation
- Plots of rating history

> The methodology is admittedly unscientific: tuned until de Cesaris, Schumacher, Ascari and Stroll landed where they should.
